PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

Applied Innovations Hub is currently in an early launch phase. This page explains the website-level data behavior of the current prototype.

Information you provide

The current project and event-interest forms may temporarily store the details you enter in your own browser so the prototype can preserve a local intake experience. Those entries are not a substitute for a connected CRM or production lead system.

Scheduling

When you open the scheduling experience, you may be directed to or shown an external scheduling service. Information submitted there is handled under that provider's privacy terms.

Local preferences

The site may use browser storage for interface preferences such as light/dark mode, dismissing an exit-intent message, or temporary prototype form data.

Analytics and production integrations

Analytics, CRM, advertising, chat, payment, and additional integrations will be documented here when they are connected to the production site.

Questions

Use the project-intake or scheduling experience on the main site for privacy-related questions until a dedicated public company contact is published.
