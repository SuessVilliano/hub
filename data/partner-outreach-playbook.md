# AIH Partner Outreach Playbook

The goal is not to cold-call and dump every AIH service on a business.

The goal is to identify one leak in the business and connect it to one practical system.

## Core Opener

"Hey, this is [NAME] with Applied Innovations Hub. We help businesses stop losing leads and time through AI and automation. I had one quick question—what happens right now when a new lead calls or messages you and nobody can respond immediately?"

Then listen.

## If They Miss Calls

"That's exactly why I called. We can build an AI receptionist that answers common questions, collects the lead information, and gets qualified people booked even when your team isn't available. Would you be open to seeing how that would work for your business?"

Offer:
24/7 AI Receptionist + Missed-Call Rescue

## If Follow-Up Is Slow

"Where do those leads go once they come in? Is somebody manually remembering to text and call everybody?"

Offer:
Business Operating System

## If They Have Old Leads

"Do you have a database of people who inquired before but never bought or booked?"

Offer:
Lead Reactivation Engine

## If Their Website Is Weak

"Does your website actually create a contact, start follow-up, and book people—or is it mostly just information?"

Offer:
Conversion Website + Funnel

## If Reviews Are Weak

"How are you asking happy customers for reviews right now?"

Offer:
Review + Reputation Engine

## If They Sell Products

"Where are you storing, packing, labeling, and handling returns right now?"

Offer:
Commerce + Fulfillment

## If They Need Local Delivery

"Are deliveries scheduled manually, and how do customers know where the delivery is?"

Offer:
Logistics + Last Mile

## Call-to-Action

Do not over-explain.

Use:
"I don't want to guess at what you need. We can do a quick Systems Audit, map where the leak is, and show you what we'd automate first. Want me to get you a time?"

## Qualification Notes to Enter in CRM

- Business name
- Contact
- Problem
- Current process
- Cost of problem if known
- Desired outcome
- Urgency
- Decision-maker?
- Next step
- Appointment date

## Avoid

- Saying "we do everything"
- Leading with government contracting
- Listing all six business divisions
- Quoting custom pricing before discovery
- Promising revenue
- Overusing AI buzzwords
- Pitching technology before identifying the operational problem

Sell the outcome:
- fewer missed leads
- faster response
- more booked appointments
- less manual work
- cleaner operations
- more visibility
- better follow-up
