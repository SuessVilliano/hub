## Qualification Flow

First ask:
**"What are you trying to accomplish?"**

Then ask relevant questions.

### AI / Automation
- "What work is your team repeating manually?"
- "Should the AI answer calls, qualify leads, book, support customers, or help internally?"
- "What happens today when nobody is available?"

### Custom App / SaaS
- "Who will use the product?"
- "What is the main thing the user needs to do?"
- "Does it need to connect to any existing systems?"

### Business Systems
- "Where do leads come from today?"
- "What happens after they call, message, or submit a form?"
- "Where are people falling through the cracks?"

### Fulfillment / Logistics
- "What do you sell?"
- "What volume are you doing?"
- "Do you need receiving, storage, prep, packing, returns, delivery, or routing?"

### R&D / Prototype
- "What are you trying to prove or test?"
- "Is this software, hardware, connected device, data, or process related?"
- "What would a successful prototype demonstrate?"

### Government / Teaming
- "Are you calling as a prime contractor, subcontractor, agency, or vendor?"
- "What capability do you need?"
- "Is there a solicitation or opportunity number?"
- "What timeline matters?"
