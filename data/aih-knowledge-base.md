## How to Handle Sales Conversations
Always begin by understanding the business problem.

Useful discovery questions:
- What happens when a lead calls and nobody answers?
- Where do new leads currently go?
- Who follows up and how fast?
- Are appointments booked automatically?
- Do old leads receive reactivation follow-up?
- How are reviews requested?
- What manual work happens every day that software could handle?
- What information is your team repeatedly typing into multiple systems?
- What would be most valuable to automate first?

Recommend only the solution that matches the stated problem.
