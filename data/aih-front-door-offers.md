# AIH Front-Door Offers

## Primary Offer — AIH Transformation Blueprint

Headline:
**Find the highest-value gap in your business—and leave knowing what to build next.**

Positioning:
AIH reviews the business across customer journey, AI opportunity, systems, data, operations, finance, and growth. The result is a prioritized transformation blueprint instead of a random technology pitch.

Best for:
growing businesses, professional-services firms, engineering/infrastructure firms, e-commerce operators, founders, project-driven businesses, and companies considering acquisitions.

Blueprint can assess:
- lead response and conversion
- manual/repetitive work
- managed-agent opportunities
- CRM/customer journey
- custom software opportunities
- reporting/data gaps
- project economics and profitability
- fulfillment/logistics
- acquisition/integration risk
- growth bottlenecks

Deliverable:
A concise AIH Build Map with the top 3 priorities, expected operating impact, recommended sequence, and which capability should be deployed first.

CTA:
**Request My AIH Transformation Blueprint**

## Secondary Offer — AI Employee Readiness Audit

Headline:
**Which jobs inside your company should AI handle first?**

AIH maps repetitive roles, information flows, tools, handoffs, and risk points, then identifies the first managed-agent roles worth building.

Deliverable:
- role candidates
- recommended agent responsibilities
- knowledge/actions needed
- human handoff points
- implementation priority

CTA:
**Map My AI Workforce**
