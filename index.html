PHYSICAL OPERATIONS

The space is part of the system.

Our planned Delaware innovation hub is being designed around real operating flow—not decoration. A quiet execution room supports focused trading, analysis, client work, and reception when needed. The open industrial floor supports fulfillment, R&D, logistics, training, content, equipment staging, and future technical buildouts.

  • Execution Room: focused workstation cluster + client/reception mode
  • Fulfillment Wall: inventory, FBA, e-commerce prep, shipping
  • R&D Wall: prototyping, testing, electronics, software/hardware work
  • Flexible Floor: workshops, training, product staging, vehicle/detailing use
  • Operations Backbone: dispatch, content, analytics, AutoBid, customer systems
