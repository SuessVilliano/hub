FAQ

Ask better questions.

Or open the AIH Assistant in the corner and ask directly.

What exactly is Applied Innovations Hub?

AIH is being built as an applied-technology and operating company—not a traditional coworking space. The model combines AI/business systems, R&D, fulfillment, logistics, training, and commercial/public-sector execution.

Can I hire AIH to automate my business?

Yes. Business infrastructure is one of the first commercial service lines: CRM setup, AI agents, lead capture, follow-up, booking, payments, reviews, customer communications, reporting, and automation.

Can you handle Amazon FBA or e-commerce fulfillment?

AIH is developing a micro-fulfillment model that can support inventory receiving, prep, packaging, kitting, returns, FBA workflows, and shipment staging. Capacity and final service levels will depend on the physical operating setup.

Can AIH subcontract under a larger company?

That is a core part of the longer-term model. AIH can scope technology, fulfillment, logistics, operational systems, sourcing, and project support opportunities while formal registrations and contract-specific requirements are completed.

Is the Delaware facility already open?

The hub is currently being planned and designed. The website intentionally separates current service capability from future facility-specific claims so the public information stays accurate.
